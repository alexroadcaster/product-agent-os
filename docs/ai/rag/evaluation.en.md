# Evaluating retrieval and answer quality

Source status: repository_source / static_contract_review; 2026-11-15.

Owner: retrieval/graph owner + docs-maintainer. Scope: DG05, revision `61f6cd4cd048be478e88e2406c733f98b60bc434`. Update when index, filters, budgets, provenance or eval contract changes. Test links do not claim a new run.

[semantic](https://github.com/alexroadcaster/product-agent-os/blob/61f6cd4cd048be478e88e2406c733f98b60bc434/plugins/product-agent-os/scripts/product_agent_os/project_graph_semantic.py) and [hybrid](https://github.com/alexroadcaster/product-agent-os/blob/61f6cd4cd048be478e88e2406c733f98b60bc434/plugins/product-agent-os/scripts/product_agent_os/project_graph_hybrid_retrieval.py) provide deterministic quality evaluation over cases with expected/unexpected IDs: semantic measures recall@k, MRR and false-positive rate; hybrid measures precision@k, MRR, NDCG and false-positive rate against thresholds. Time and approximate payload tokens are measured for a particular run. [quality tests](https://github.com/alexroadcaster/product-agent-os/blob/61f6cd4cd048be478e88e2406c733f98b60bc434/tests/product_agent_os/test_project_graph_search_quality.py) and [semantic tests](https://github.com/alexroadcaster/product-agent-os/blob/61f6cd4cd048be478e88e2406c733f98b60bc434/tests/product_agent_os/test_project_graph_semantic.py) are fixture/regression routes; links are not a new run or real-project measurement.

[embedding gate](https://github.com/alexroadcaster/product-agent-os/blob/61f6cd4cd048be478e88e2406c733f98b60bc434/plugins/product-agent-os/scripts/product_agent_os/project_graph_embedding_provider.py) compares supplied provider result evidence to baseline and thresholds, but a ready gate does not imply a live call. Missing/failed eval leaves provider disabled with its stated fallback; [provider tests](https://github.com/alexroadcaster/product-agent-os/blob/61f6cd4cd048be478e88e2406c733f98b60bc434/tests/product_agent_os/test_project_graph_embedding_provider.py). Future provider eval requires separately authorized data, budgets, access and actual evidence. M5 pgvector and external PoC remain planned.

Retrieval quality concerns relevance and coverage of retrieved pointers. Answer quality also requires checking sources, context sufficiency, contradictions and unsupported claims; retrieval metrics alone do not assess an answer. End-to-end Web RAG and a provider quality baseline are not established by this change.
