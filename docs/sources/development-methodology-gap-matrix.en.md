## How To Read This Matrix

`Covered` means the claim already has a Product-Agent OS artifact and a verification surface.  
`Partial` means the claim exists in prose or a template but lacks a strong gate, discovery signal or fixture coverage.  
`Missing` means the claim is part of research but has not yet become plugin behavior.
