## Boundaries

- ASVS/OWASP mapping does not claim GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, PCI, SOC 2 or enterprise compliance certification.
- Record explicit not-applicable rationale for controls outside scope.
- Link to the security risk review, `api-security-review.md`, `authz-negative-test-matrix.md` and `compliance-overlay.md` where relevant.

Do not include raw secrets, credentials, access tokens, private keys, payment data, raw PII, raw private chats or private customer data.
