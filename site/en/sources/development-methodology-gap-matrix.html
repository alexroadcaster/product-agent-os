    Gap Matrix: Source Claim -> Plugin Artifact -> Eval/Check -> Residual Risk¶

    Source status: official + verified methodology + implementation audit

    Analysis date: 2026-05-23.
    Scope: Product-Agent OS after Track 7, including .codex/workflows/development-standards.md, .codex/workflows/documentation-system.md, .codex/artifact-templates/*.md, project-readiness.json, workspace discovery signals and lifecycle skills.

    How To Read This Matrix¶

    Covered means the claim already has a Product-Agent OS artifact and a verification surface.
    Partial means the claim exists in prose or a template but lacks a strong gate, discovery signal or fixture coverage.
    Missing means the claim is part of research but has not yet become plugin behavior.
