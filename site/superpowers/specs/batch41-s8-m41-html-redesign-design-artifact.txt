## Design Direction

Artifact type: `technical architecture dossier / operational cockpit`.

Primary artifact policy: one self-contained `index.html` remains the primary artifact. Any multi-page diagnostic output is supporting evidence, not the owner-facing product surface.

Design language:

- light operational dossier, not dark marketing tech;
- off-white paper background, near-black ink, white panels, restrained green/teal accent;
- stable semantic colors for status, not decorative color cycling;
- system sans for prose and `ui-monospace` for paths, hashes, statuses and counts;
- 8px panel radius, pill radius only for navigation/status chips;
- high-density grids, ledgers and diagram panels;
- no external fonts, CDN, external scripts, hosted visual services or UI-library runtime by default.
